Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 September 2026

Car Ledger is an iPhone app published by Albatros Solutions ("we"). This policy explains what the app stores and what happens to it. The short version: everything you enter stays on your iPhone, and we never receive any of it.

We collect nothing

Car Ledger has no user accounts, no sign-in, no server of its own, and contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting code. The app makes no network requests. We, and any third party, have no access to your data.

What the app stores on your device

The data you enter is saved in the app's private storage on your iPhone, inside the app sandbox that iOS protects. It includes:

  • vehicle details — name, make, model, year, licence plate, VIN, odometer readings and free-form notes;
  • a photo of the vehicle, if you add one, and the colour you choose for its card;
  • service records — date, odometer, category, cost, notes and any photos you attach;
  • fuel entries and oil top-ups, with dates, volumes, odometer values and costs;
  • maintenance reminders and the service intervals you set;
  • documents you add — registration papers, receipts, manuals, photos and other files you import;
  • notes and fault codes you record, with optional photos;
  • your settings — language, appearance, units and currency.

None of this leaves the device through the app. It is included in your iPhone's own backups (iCloud backup or an encrypted computer backup) if you have those turned on; those backups are handled by Apple under Apple's terms, not by us.

Camera and photo library

You can attach photos to a vehicle, a service record or a note. If you choose the camera, iOS asks for camera permission and the photo you take is saved inside the app. If you choose an existing photo, iOS shows its own picker and hands the app only the images you pick — the app never browses your photo library. You can decline or revoke either permission in Settings; the rest of the app keeps working.

Files you import

When you add a document, iOS shows the system file picker and the app copies only the file you chose into its own storage.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Car Ledger schedules reminders locally on the device for upcoming maintenance. The reminders are created by the app on your iPhone; no push server is involved and no notification data is sent anywhere.

Exporting your data

In Garage → Data you can export the service, fuel and oil logs as CSV files, export a PDF service history, and back up everything — including photos and documents — to a single JSON file. The iOS share sheet then lets you decide where that file goes: Files, Mail, another app. What happens to it after that is up to you and the app or service you send it to. The same screen restores a backup from a JSON file you pick.

Deleting your data

Every record — a service entry, fuel entry, oil top-up, reminder, note, document or a whole vehicle — can be deleted inside the app. Deleting the app from your iPhone removes all of its data with it. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for you to request from us and nothing for us to delete on our side. If you have kept device backups, remove them through Apple's own controls.

Children's privacy

Car Ledger is a tool for vehicle owners and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Your rights

Data-protection laws give you rights to access, correct, export and delete your personal data. In Car Ledger you exercise all of them directly: the data is on your device, you can view and edit every record, export it in CSV, PDF or JSON form, and delete it. We hold no copy, so we cannot access, change or disclose it, and there is nothing we could hand over in response to a request.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes what it stores or adds any service that leaves the device, this page will be updated before that version ships, with a new effective date.
