What Car Ledger does

Service log

Record each job with date, odometer, cost, category, notes and photos. Filter the log by category and export a PDF service history.

Fuel and consumption

Log fill-ups and see average consumption in L/100 km, km/L, mpg (US) or mpg (UK).

Oil top-ups

Track oil added between changes and how much the engine uses over distance.

Maintenance reminders

Set reminders by distance or date, with editable service intervals. Local notifications warn you a week ahead and on the day. Mark one done and it becomes a service record.

Spending chart

See what the car costs by month (day by day), six months, a year, or a date range you pick.

Documents

Keep the registration, receipts, manuals and photos with the car and open them in a single tap.

Notes and fault codes

Jot down notes or warning-light codes, and mark them resolved when fixed.

Several cars

Add as many vehicles as you like, each with its own photo or colour card, plate, VIN and mileage.

Your language, your units

English, Russian and Ukrainian; kilometres or miles; litres or gallons; a choice of currencies; light, dark or automatic appearance.

Export and backup

Export the service, fuel and oil logs as CSV, or back up everything — photos and documents included — to a single JSON file you keep.
